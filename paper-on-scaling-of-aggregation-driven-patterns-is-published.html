Paper on scaling of aggregation-driven patterns is published

  • Mon 02 November 2026
  • misc

Our paper on aggregation-driven patterning and scaling is now out in PNAS! Cell-cell interactions drive aggregation, which leads to patterns of Brachyury expression. These patterns scale over a range of system sizes, but this scaling breaks down for large systems. We show how a theoretical model with simple assumptions (but a nontrivial mathematical form) explains most of our experimental observations. This paper is the result of a really nice combination between experiment and theory from Erzberger and Aulehla labs at EMBL, continued (on my side) at ULB. In particular, we managed to quantitatively compare experimental results with theoretical predictions - something not often done in the biological pattern formation field.
